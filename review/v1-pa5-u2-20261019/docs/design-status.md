# Implementation and design boundary

## Included trial implementation

The core has five chapters with alternative combat/facility/event paths rather than a mandatory fixed fight count. Events use immutable offers and their own origin; an event boss does not advance a progression milestone. Fleeing does not grant combat rewards. Ordinary training can remain in its finite visit and append preparation only for the newly eligible purchased unit, with no general restock.

Each awakened unit retains two basic skills plus a distinct ultimate action. Multiple units may awaken after their own second-class completion,60G preparation and matching retained key; there is no chapter eligibility gate and no global single-unit awakening cap. The shipped numerical effects and scheduling are trial configuration. Receipt validation, occupancy/resource limits, terminal knockout accounting, atomic rollback and cold restoration are mechanism boundaries, not proof of natural difficulty.

The interest relic freezes ownership and principal at battle start and settles only with the authoritative successful battle transaction. Visits, escape and repeated commits do not add income. Current trial figures are20G principal per1G interest with a5G cap. Permanent profile currency is separate and its new issuance/purchase APIs remain disabled.

Family weighting retains distinct promoted owned units, including reserves, and samples eligible unowned family keys without a manual target guarantee. The current25% key gate can dilute ordinary high-grade offers. This is a known trial consequence, not a final rarity design decision.

## Not implemented here

-120-item relic catalog and strengthened legendary ultimate keys.
-New1–5chapter rarity tables or a shared total legendary budget. Proposed figures are not adopted.
-Additional class branches or revised encounter-pattern catalog.
-U3 follow-up UI work, production publication or a new profile-token economy.

The proposed catalog target is17 common,34 uncommon,31 rare,24 epic and14 legendary, including4 ultimate-key relics in that legendary count. This is a design target, not the current executable26-item registry. Catalog effects, prices, effective eligibility and probabilities need a separate versioned implementation and actual-path evaluation. A rare fixed-event grant must not become an accidental legendary-key bypass during such a change.
